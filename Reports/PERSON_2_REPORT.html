4. 3D Spatial Geometry & Coordinate System

The system operates in the Earth-Centered Inertial (ECI) coordinate frame:

  • Origin $(0, 0, 0)$: Geocenter of Earth.
  • Reference Sphere: Mean Earth radius $R_E = 6371.0\text{ km}$.
  • Altitude Calculation: $h = \|\vec{r}\| - R_E = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2} - 6371.0\text{ km}$.
  • True 3D Motion: Replaces the 2D projection limitation of Person 1 with full $X, Y, Z$ coordinates.

7. Encounter Event Persistence (Task 4)

When an encounter breaches the critical threshold ($\le 10\text{ km}$), an event record is latched:

  • The minimum Closest Approach Distance (CAD) and Time of Closest Approach (TCA) are permanently logged.
  • Even after the objects diverge and separation exceeds $100\text{ km}$ (returning real-time status to SAFE), the HUD maintains the persisted encounter event alert.
